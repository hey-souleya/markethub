3. 報名表常見用語3. Words you'll see on application forms

4. 繳費小提醒4. Paying the booth fee

  • 大多數主辦方用台灣的銀行轉帳收費；可以用什麼方式付款，以各主辦方的說明為準。Most organizers take a Taiwan bank transfer; check each organizer's payment instructions.
  • 「後五碼」通常是指你轉出那個帳戶的最後五碼。轉帳後記得截圖留存。"後五碼" usually means the last 5 digits of the account you paid from. Screenshot your receipt.
  • 付款前先看主辦方的「退費規定」。Read the organizer's refund policy before you pay.
  • 報名前先看看主辦方 IG／FB 過去辦過的活動，避免遇到假的市集。可疑的話可以打 165 反詐騙專線。Check the organizer's past events on IG/FB before applying to avoid fake markets. Taiwan's anti-fraud hotline is 165.

5. 海外品牌參展5. Overseas brands at expos

各展會海外品牌的報名方式不同，請看「大型展會」分頁每張卡片的說明，再點進主辦方官網確認。Each expo handles overseas applicants differently. See the notes on each card in the Expos tab, then confirm on the organizer's site.
